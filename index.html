Efficient deep learning · Neural architecture search · Model compression

Email GitHub Publication Portfolio

I make neural networks cheaper to run without losing accuracy. My work covers searching for efficient architectures, removing redundant computation after search, and checking whether the cheap signals used to guide search can be trusted.

I am finishing a B.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Kyonggi University, where I have been an undergraduate researcher in the AutoML Lab (advisor: Prof. Wangduk Seo). In March 2027 I start an M.S. at the AutoML Lab, Chung-Ang University, working on joint pruning, quantization, and low-rank compression for on-device LLMs.
